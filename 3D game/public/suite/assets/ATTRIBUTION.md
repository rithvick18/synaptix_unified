## Models

All 76 assets are procedural geometry built in `src/suite/assets/builders/` (original work for this project, CC0-1.0). No external 3D model files are used.

## Thumbnails

`thumbs/<id>.webp` are rendered from those procedural builders by `tools/suite/assets/render-thumbnails.mjs` (project-generated, CC0-1.0).

## Fonts, pictures and sounds

- Fonts: see `public/suite/fonts/ATTRIBUTION.md` (Noto Sans Devanagari, OFL-1.1).
- Demo pictures and synthesized sounds in `public/suite/packs/*/`: project-generated by `tools/suite/content/`, CC0-1.0, recorded per file in each pack's `content.json`. The pictures are illustrations, not photographs of real people, places or events.
